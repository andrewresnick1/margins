How to play

Each day has five numbers from the news: polls, money, votes, markets. Drag the slider to your guess and lock it in. You score up to 100 per round, losing points the further you land from the real number. Within about 2% of the slider's range is a bullseye. Everyone gets the same five numbers, and every answer links to the story it came from. New numbers arrive at midnight Eastern.
