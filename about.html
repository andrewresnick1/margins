About Margin

Margin is a free daily game about the numbers behind the news. Each day brings five real figures from recent headlines: poll results, campaign money, vote counts, markets, and world data. You guess each one with a slider, and you score by how close you land.

Where the numbers come from

Every answer comes from a published news report or primary source, and the game links to it after you guess. Questions are written to be fair to readers of any political leaning. We test what the numbers are, not what you should think about them.

Spot an error?

If a number looks wrong or a source has been updated, email hello@margin.example.com with the date and round. Corrections are made the same day.

Who makes it

Margin is an independent project made by one person in Connecticut who reads too much news.
