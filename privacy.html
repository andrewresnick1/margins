Privacy

Last updated October 19, 2026

No accounts

You can play Margin without an account, name, or email address. Your guesses, scores, and streak are saved only in your own browser's local storage. Clearing your site data erases them. We never receive them.

Analytics

We use privacy-friendly analytics to count visits and completed games, along with browser type, device type, and approximate country. We don't use this to identify you.

Advertising

Margin may show ads served by a third-party ad partner. Ad partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including ads based on your interests. Our ad partner's own privacy policy governs that data, and this page will name the partner once ads are live. You can opt out of interest-based ads at optout.aboutads.info.

Children

Margin is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.
